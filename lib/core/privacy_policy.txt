Savvy Lite – Privacy Policy

Last updated: [12 Dec 2025]

This Privacy Policy explains how Tech Equations we collect,
uses, and protects information when you use the Savvy Lite mobile app. Savvy Lite is intended for business use by companies
and their authorized staff.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.
If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Scope

1.1 Platforms covered
This Policy applies to your use of Savvy Lite on Android and on any other
platforms where Savvy Lite is made available, unless stated otherwise.

1.2 Business use
Savvy Lite is designed for use by organizations (for example, companies running
stock and sales operations). It is not directed to private consumers or
children.

2. Information we collect

The App itself does not collect data for advertising or marketing purposes.
However, in order to provide stock and sales management functionality to your
organization, the App may process the following categories of information:

2.1 User and account information
- User identifiers such as username, employee ID, role, and access privileges.
- Business contact details such as name, email address, or phone number, when
  entered into the system by your organization.
- Authentication-related data (for example, hashed or encrypted credentials or
  tokens used to sign in to the App or to your backend systems).

2.2 Customer and supplier information
When your organization uses Savvy Lite to manage business partners, the App may
store:
- Customer information (names, contact details, account numbers, credit terms,
  and other data your organization chooses to record).
- Supplier information (names, contact details, payment/banking details where
  entered, and related records).

All such information is entered and controlled by your organization, which acts
as the data controller for that data.

2.3 Inventory, sales, and transaction data
To support stock and sales management, the App may process:
- Item and inventory data (item codes, descriptions, units of measure, pricing,
  cost information, item locations, and branches).
- Stock transactions (purchases, sales, returns, transfers, and adjustments).
- Sales documents such as quotations, sales orders, invoices, and credit
  receipts.
- Purchase documents such as purchase orders and related records.
- Payment or credit-related information associated with the above documents,
  where configured by your organization.

2.4 Device and usage information
To operate reliably, the App may process certain technical data, such as:
- Device information (for example, device model and operating system version).
- App information (App version and basic diagnostic logs used for debugging and
  improving stability).
- Connectivity state (for example, whether the device is online or offline) to
  allow data synchronization when a network connection is available.

The App does not request or use precise location, contact lists, call logs, or
SMS messages.

2.5 Files and documents
If your organization uses import/export features (for example, working with
Excel files and reports), the App may:
- Access files that you explicitly select through a file picker in order to
  import data into the App.
- Create and write files (for example, reports or exports) to storage
  locations that you choose.

The App does not access other files on the device that you have not explicitly
selected for import or export.

2.6 Camera data (QR/barcode scanning)
If the QR or barcode scanning features are enabled:
- The App uses the device camera solely to scan item barcodes or QR codes.
- Images from the camera are processed locally to read the code and are not
  stored for unrelated purposes.
- The App does not use the camera for facial recognition or other biometric
  identification.

3. How we use information

We use the information described above only for purposes related to providing
and supporting the App and your organization’s business processes, including:

3.1 To provide the App and its functionality
- Authenticating users and enforcing role-based access control.
- Displaying and updating inventory, customers, suppliers, sales, purchases,
  and related records.
- Enabling QR/barcode scanning, file import/export, and other in-App features.

3.2 To synchronize with backend systems
- Communicating with your organization’s backend servers or ERP system (via
  HTTP or HTTPS) to send and receive the data necessary for operations, such as
  items, orders, invoices, and user information.

3.3 To maintain, secure, and improve the App
- Diagnosing technical issues and errors.
- Improving performance, reliability, and user experience.
- Protecting user accounts and preventing unauthorized access.

We do not use data collected through the App to deliver third-party ads or to
build advertising profiles of end users.

4. Legal bases (where applicable)

Depending on your location and applicable data protection laws (for example,
the GDPR in the European Economic Area and the UK), we may rely on different
legal bases for processing data, including:
- Performance of a contract: To provide the App and fulfill our obligations to
  your organization.
- Legitimate interests: To operate, maintain, and improve the App, and to
  ensure security, provided that such interests are not overridden by your
  rights and freedoms.
- Legal obligations: To comply with laws and regulations.
- Consent: Where your organization or users give explicit consent for a
  particular processing activity, if applicable.

Your organization is responsible for determining its own legal bases for
processing business data using Savvy Lite.

5. Data storage and security

5.1 Local storage on the device
- The App uses a local database (for example, SQLite via sqflite) and shared
  preferences to store data needed for offline usage and performance.
- Sensitive information, such as authentication tokens or credentials (if
  stored), may be kept in secure storage and protected using industry-standard
  encryption mechanisms.

5.2 Server-side storage
- When online, the App communicates with your organization’s backend systems or
  servers.
- The retention, backup, and security of data stored on those servers are
  managed by your organization and/or [Your Company Name] according to the
  agreements and policies that apply to you.

5.3 Security measures
- We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data
  against accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized
  disclosure, or access.
- No system is completely secure; therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute
  security of your data.

6. Data sharing and transfers

6.1 Within your organization
Data entered or processed in Savvy Lite may be accessible to other authorized
users and systems within your organization, according to the roles and
permissions you configure.

6.2 Third-party service providers
- We do not sell personal or business data to third parties.
- We may use third-party service providers (for example, hosting, backup, or
  monitoring providers) to help us operate and maintain the App. These
  providers may process data on our behalf under contractual obligations that
  require them to protect your information.

6.3 Legal requirements
We may disclose data if required to do so by law, regulation, or legal process,
or in response to valid requests from public authorities.

6.4 International transfers
If information is transferred outside your country (for example, to servers in
other jurisdictions), we will take appropriate steps to ensure that the data is
protected in accordance with applicable law and this Privacy Policy.

7. Data retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to:
- Provide the App and related services.
- Fulfill our contractual obligations to your organization.
- Comply with applicable legal, accounting, or reporting obligations.

Your organization may determine how long certain types of business data (such
as transactions, items, and customer or supplier records) are kept in its
systems. Where required by law, we or your organization may delete or
anonymize data or honor valid requests to do so.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you and/or your organization may
have certain rights in relation to personal data processed through the App,
such as:
- The right to access your personal data.
- The right to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
- The right to request deletion of certain data, subject to legal or
  contractual limitations.
- The right to restrict or object to certain types of processing.
- The right to data portability (to receive certain data in a structured,
  commonly used, and machine-readable format).

If you are an end user (for example, an employee using the App as part of your
job), please contact your organization’s administrator first for any requests

9. Children’s privacy

Savvy Lite is not directed to children and is intended for use by businesses
and their authorized personnel only. We do not knowingly collect personal
information from children. If you believe that we have unintentionally
collected information about a child, please contact us so that we can take
appropriate steps to remove that information.

10. Permissions used by the App

The exact permissions requested by your installation of Savvy Lite may vary
depending on configuration and platform, but typically include:

10.1 Camera
- Purpose: To scan barcodes or QR codes on items and documents in order to
  quickly retrieve or enter item information.
- We do not use the camera for any purpose other than code scanning within the
  App.

10.2 Network / Internet access
- Purpose: To communicate with your organization’s backend servers or ERP
  system so that data such as items, orders, invoices, and user information can
  be synchronized.
- Where configured, data in transit should be protected using HTTPS or similar
  encryption.

10.3 File and storage access (if enabled)
- Purpose: To allow users to import data from files they choose (for example,
  Excel spreadsheets) and to export reports or other documents to locations
  selected by the user.
- The App does not access or modify other files on your device that you have
  not explicitly selected through the App.

If your production version of Savvy Lite does not use certain permissions,
those permissions will not be requested. If you or your organization enable new
permissions or features in the future, this Privacy Policy and your app store
declarations (including Google Play’s Data safety section) should be updated to
reflect those changes.

11. Third-party libraries and services

Savvy Lite uses various third-party libraries and frameworks (for example,
Flutter packages) to provide necessary functionality such as database access,
network communication, cryptography, secure storage, QR/barcode scanning, and
file handling.

These libraries operate as part of the App and do not independently sell your
data for advertising purposes. Any external services that your organization
connects to (for example, its own ERP, APIs, or other third-party systems) are
subject to their own privacy policies and terms, which are separate from this
Privacy Policy.
